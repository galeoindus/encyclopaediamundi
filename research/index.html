Research desk

For a question that deserves more than a paragraph: the answer broken into topics, every sentence traceable.

What the desk gives you

  • A direct answer, then the key sentences that carry it.
  • Numbered topics, each one a section of the source article.
  • The source, with the licence it is used under.
  • Markdown export, so the notes can leave the browser.

Where it reads from

Wikipedia in twelve languages, with Wikidata for structured facts and Wikimedia Commons for images and their licences.

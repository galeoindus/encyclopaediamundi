# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, the sources and the evidence, never about the
person you are talking to.

## What is expected

- **Cite your claim.** This is a site about sourced answers; the same standard
  applies to a pull request. "It feels wrong" is a starting point, not an
  argument — say what you observed and where.
- **Assume the other person is acting in good faith.** Most disagreements here
  are about scope or taste, not competence.
- **Respect the licence boundary.** Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA 4.0 and must keep
  its attribution. Wikidata is CC0. An image carries whatever its uploader
  chose. Do not strip a credit to make a page look tidier.
- **Keep the site's promise.** Veridell says *every answer, with its source.* A
  change that lets the product present an uncited claim as sourced is out of
  scope, however good the demo looks.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language about any person or group,
deliberate intimidation, publishing someone's private information, or sustained
disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and any
space where you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer at the address in [SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md) if the
matter is sensitive, or open a private report through the repository's security
advisory page. Reports are read, acknowledged, and acted on. Retaliation against
someone who reports in good faith is itself a violation.
